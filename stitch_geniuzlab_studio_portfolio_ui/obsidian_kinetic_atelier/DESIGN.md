## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the high-voltage elegance of an avant-garde digital atelier. It pairs cinematic darkroom immersion with surgical neo-grotesque clarity, built for forward-thinking creative studios, production houses, and cutting-edge tech labs. The atmosphere is nocturnal, hypnotic, and calculated: undulating 3D silk and metallic ribbons glide beneath the surface, illuminated by shocks of radiant electric lime and emerald phosphor.

The design movement converges **Cinematic Minimalism** with **Tactile Glassmorphism**. UI surfaces do not rely on flat, sterile containers; instead, they exist as smoked acrylic lenses, deep moss-tinted obsidian substrates, and translucent planes bound by crisp 1px hairline edges. Visual hierarchy is established through extreme contrast—pairing impenetrable blacks with mint-tinted bone typography and hyper-saturated fluorescent accents that simulate glowing diodes in a pitch-black gallery.

The emotional impression is elite, daring, and technologically advanced:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, enigmatic, kinetic, and refined.
- **Audience:** Global founders, creative visionaries, design connoisseurs, and tech innovators seeking museum-grade execution.
- **Experience:** Smooth spring-loaded interactions, tactile pills, floating dock navigation, and spatial depth reminiscent of boutique hardware interfaces.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an ultra-deep black base with high-chroma botanical radiation. The primary contrast engine matches rich, non-fatiguing obsidian blacks with electric lime and soft mint-white typography.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Obsidian Black Canvas (`#07090A` / `#050505`):** The primary void. Used as the global viewport background, grounding the entire interface in cinematic dark space.
- **Subterranean Cinema (`#10140D`):** An organic dark green-black utilized for elevated cards, feature callouts, and backdrop panels.
- **Dark Moss Surface (`#12140F`):** The default container background for interactive surfaces, lists, input fields, and floating capsules.
- **Elevated Capsule Surface (`#0E120A`):** The darkest container shade, dedicated to resting pill tracks and elevator dials.

### Primary Accents & Energy
- **Radiant Electric Lime (`#7ED957`):** The primary beacon. Used for active badges, glowing halos, primary button fills, switch indicators, and key metric displays.
- **Emerald Accent (`#52D643`):** The secondary chromatic driver. Used for gradient transitions, success telemetry, and interactive hover rings.
- **Brass Olive (`#8BC34A`):** The tertiary grounding lime. Applied to subtle borders, solid CTAs, and active icon accents.

### Typography & Linework
- **Crisp Mint Ivory (`#F1F7EA`):** High-contrast display headlines, card labels, and primary reading copy.
- **Muted Sage (`#93A389`):** Secondary metadata, inactive states, descriptors, and subtitle copy.
- **Hairline Border Glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): The standard 1px container boundary across dark surfaces.

### Light Mode Alternative
When running in light mode, the canvas shifts to a crisp ivory surface (`#F9F9F7`), containers convert to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by hairline zinc lines (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`), and text renders in deep charcoal olive (`#10140D`). Radiant Electric Lime (`#7ED957`) and vibrant emeralds remain intact as the signature interactive punch.

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts structural geometric sans-serifs with tight, punchy headlines and technical monospace tags.

- **Headlines (Outfit):** Delivers clean neo-grotesque authority. Display headlines utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and condensed line-heights to produce striking, editorial poster titles.
- **Body Text (Geist):** Handles descriptive prose, client bios, form labels, and interactive copy. Set with generous line-heights for rapid scanability on dark backdrops, keeping paragraph measures capped between 45 and 55 characters.
- **Tags & Metadata (Space Grotesk):** Provides technical studio DNA. Used in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.14em` to `0.18em`) for category eyebrow chips, client timestamps, and project scopes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a centered single-frame layout that scales seamlessly from a focused mobile container to a wide editorial stage.

### Form Factors & Grids
- **Mobile Viewport (`< 768px`):** Max width of `460px` centered with `16px` margins. Layout conforms to a single vertical column with dense, tactile card modules.
- **Tablet / Small Desktop (`768px - 1024px`):** Max width of `720px` to `880px`. Cards reflow into dynamic 2-column asymmetric grids.
- **Editorial Canvas (`> 1024px`):** Max width of `1280px` to `1440px`. Expands full-bleed showcase visuals with sticky metadata rails and wide project tiles.

### Spacing Principles
All spacing adheres strictly to a 4px/8px modular cadence:
- Component internal padding uses compact increments (`space-sm` for chips, `space-md` for row items, `space-lg` for large cards).
- Between consecutive content segments (e.g., hero titles to button groups), maintain an intentional gap of `20px` to `28px` to preserve tight visual gravity.
- Full section dividers incorporate generous spatial pauses (`48px` to `80px`) to evoke gallery pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through stacked acrylic strata, ambient neon emissions, and frosted backdrops rather than conventional drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Planes
1. **Backdrop Void (Level 0):** Obsidian black (`#07090A`) overlaid with an undulating dark metallic ribbon layer and an architectural 16px dot matrix (`radial-gradient(rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05) 1px, transparent 1px)`).
2. **Base Glass Substrate (Level 1):** Solid moss surface (`#12140F`) combined with a 1px perimeter border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Elevated Frosted Panes (Level 2):** Translucent acrylics with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), subtle top-edge specularity (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`), and low-alpha side boundaries.
4. **Active Luminescence (Level 3):** High-energy interactive nodes cast soft fluorescent neon halos:
   - Primary Halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(126, 217, 87, 0.45)`
   - Floating Dock Glow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary revolves around hyper-curved, fluid enclosures. Rounded pill capsules dominate interactive elements, creating smooth hardware-grade touch surfaces that offset the sharp geometric precision of the typography.

- **Primary Interactive Shells (Pill / 999px):** Action buttons, category tags, floating dock containers, and active indicator sliders feature full pill geometry.
- **Cards & Content Surfaces (20px - 24px / `rounded-3xl`):** Showcase cards, portfolio tiles, service panels, and modal containers use generous 20px to 24px corners, balancing friendly ergonomics with sophisticated industrial design.
- **Media Thumbnails & Icon Squirceles (12px - 14px):** Internal avatar frames, video preview slots, and service glyph enclosures use intermediate squircle rounding.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Electric Pill Button:** Full pill contour (`rounded-full`), solid background in Radiant Electric Lime (`#7ED957`), and text set in deep obsidian (`#07090A`, font-weight 700). Accompanied by a subtle neon outer glow (`0 0 20px rgba(126, 217, 87, 0.35)`). Active state applies a spring press scale (`transform: scale(0.97)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Pill Button:** Transparent surface with a 1px boundary (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text rendered in Crisp Mint Ivory (`#F1F7EA`). On hover, the border brightens to `#7ED957` with an ambient moss fill (`rgba(126, 217, 87, 0.08)`).
- **Icon Action Buttons:** Circular 44px buttons featuring centered diagonal arrow glyphs or service symbols, surrounded by a 1px hairline border.

### 2. Floating Dock Navigation (Bottom Bar)
- **Dock Enclosure:** Centered horizontal pill capsule (`max-width: 320px` to `420px`), floating 24px above the viewport base. Framed in Dark Moss Surface (`#12140F`), backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, and bound by a 1px glass perimeter (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Kinetic Active Elevator:** The active navigation item displays a sliding pill capsule or elevated circle rendered in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or luminous moss (`#0E120A`), featuring an electric lime icon indicator and smooth spring physics (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 1.4, 0.5, 1)`). Inactive slots show muted sage icons (`#93A389`).

### 3. Cards & Service Panels
- **Service List Row:** Full-width dark moss capsule (`#12140F`) with 20px corner radius, interior padding of 14px 18px. Left slot contains a squircle icon container with vibrant lime glyph; right slot features an angled directional arrow (`↗`) that translates up-right on hover.
- **Verdant Showcase Card:** Uses a rich diagonal gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #1A3311 0%, #10140D 60%, #07090A 100%)`), housing high-contrast mint titles, client monospace metadata, and embedded media assets.

### 4. Chips, Pills & Eyebrow Badges
- **Editorial Eyebrow Chip:** Pill capsule with translucent lime fill (`rgba(126, 217, 87, 0.14)`), 1px border (`rgba(126, 217, 87, 0.3)`), and high-contrast lime uppercase copy (`#7ED957`).
- **Filter Tags:** Smoked glass pills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with muted sage text (`#93A389`). Active tags swap to solid lime fill with deep black typography.

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Form Text Inputs:** Deep charcoal containers (`#0E120A`), 16px corner radius, 14px internal padding, and subtle hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). Placeholder text set in muted sage (`#93A389`). Focus triggers a vibrant 1px Electric Lime ring and 8px diffuse glow.
- **Toggle Switches:** Pill track (`#0E120A` inactive, `#7ED957` active) hosting a crisp, pure white thumb that glides with spring momentum.
